Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? expand_more
Navigate to the login screen and click on "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a secure link to reset your credentials. Links expire after 24 hours.
Can I export sales reports to CSV? expand_more
Yes. In the Sales module, select the desired date range and click the "Export" icon in the top right corner of the data table. Choose "CSV" from the dropdown menu to download your report.
How often does inventory data sync? expand_more
Inventory data syncs in real-time across all connected devices whenever a transaction is recorded. If you are offline, changes will be queued and synchronized automatically once connectivity is restored.
